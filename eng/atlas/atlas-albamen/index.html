ALBAMAN — EXPLORER FROM ALBARIS

Hear the story of the explorer from Albaris in his own words.

Home: AlbarisMy daughter: LaraMy mission: Discovery

Shae, my space friends! 🌌 I'm Albaman! Yes, that curious traveller with the red cape is me. Today I won't be telling you about another explorer. I'll be telling you my own story. Ready? Let's head beyond the stars to where it all began! 🚀

I wasn't born on Earth. My home is a distant planet called Albaris. Its skies may look very different from yours, but the desire to discover new things is something we share. The greatest power isn't speed or energy. It's curiosity and the will to learn.

I rarely travel alone. My daughter Lara often joins me. She's brave, observant and full of questions. Sometimes she spots things even I have missed! I've learned that a good question can be more powerful than any dazzling superpower.

How did we meet the people of Earth? One part of our adventure begins near Göbeklitepe, in Türkiye, where researchers discover a mysterious spacecraft. As they try to understand what they've found, an extraordinary encounter changes everything. The discovery opens a new chapter for both our worlds. 🌍

Now for the question everyone asks: What can I do? Let's begin with time. Sometimes I can sense what may happen five seconds ahead. I can also hold certain objects still for roughly eight seconds. Imagine pressing the universe's pause button for just a moment!

On some occasions, I can travel 88 seconds back in time. I can hear echoes of moments that have already passed, as though the past has left clues behind. But a second chance isn't an excuse to act carelessly. It's a chance to choose more wisely. ⏳

I can fly beyond an atmosphere, travel through space and withstand cosmic radiation. I can move at incredible speed, and my strength and resilience are far beyond those of an ordinary human. But having power doesn't mean frightening others. It means being able to help when someone needs you. 💫

My eyes can release concentrated energy beams, and I can see beneath the surfaces of some objects. I can bend light to create a shield or conceal myself. I can influence metal with magnetic fields and send out bursts of cosmic energy from my hands. Those abilities demand great responsibility.

My senses are unusually sharp. I might hear a distant sound or notice a tiny clue others would miss. One of my favourite abilities is opening portals through space. Imagine a doorway that leads to a sky you've never seen before! ✨

On Albaris we speak the Albari language. When I say “Shae”, I'm greeting you. “Vael-khrun” evokes strength and the light within us. We may speak different languages, but friendship, kindness and the joy of discovery need no translation.

People sometimes see my powers and assume I know everything. I don't. Lara keeps asking questions. The scientists of Earth keep finding answers. Every new adventure reminds me just how much there is still to learn.

So here's my message to you, space explorers: never lose your curiosity. Look at the stars, ask questions, experiment and help each other. When knowledge, imagination and kindness come together, extraordinary journeys begin. I'm Albaman, and Lara and I will see you on our next adventure. Vael-khrun🖖 🚀🌟
