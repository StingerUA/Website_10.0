ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE WILL MAP THE MOON: A NEW ERA FROM NASA AND IBM

Muzaffer Enes İpçi · Guest Writer · September 13, 2026 · 5 min read

AND A NEW ERA FROM IBM

NASA and IBM have developed a new open-source artificial intelligence model that will enable faster and more detailed examination of the Moon's surface. The system called NASA-IBM Lunar Foundation Model; It can be used to detect craters, volcanic formations and possible ice fields, especially in the polar regions, on the Moon.

A New Era in Lunar Research

A huge amount of data has been collected about the Moon for decades. However, it can take quite a long time for humans to examine all of this data one by one.

This is where the new artificial intelligence model developed by NASA and IBM comes into play.

The NASA-IBM Lunar Foundation Model, which was launched on September 10, 2026, analyzes large amounts of scientific data on the Moon's surface, helping researchers identify different geological features faster. Since the model is published as open source, it can be developed and used by researchers for different scientific studies.

What Will Artificial Intelligence Be Able to Study on the Moon?

The new model is not just a simple system designed to classify images of the Moon.

Researchers model;

  • Mapping craters on the Moon,
  • to identify young and unusual volcanic formations,
  • To investigate ice fields that may be found in the polar regions of the Moon,
  • to detect new changes occurring on the surface,
  • Better understanding the geological history of the Moon

It can be used for different studies such as.

This feature is especially important for the polar regions of the Moon.

Why is ice on the Moon so important?

In some polar regions of the Moon, there are permanently shaded areas where sunlight never reaches.

Because temperatures are so low in these regions, water ice may have been preserved for billions of years.

Water could be an extremely valuable resource for long-term human bases that may be established on the Moon in the future. Because water is important not only for drinking and life support systems, but also for obtaining oxygen and hydrogen with appropriate technologies.

Therefore, a better understanding of where and in what quantity the ice on the Moon is found could help plan future exploration missions.

How was the model trained?

The NASA-IBM model was trained using large amounts of data from different lunar missions, most notably NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO).

According to NASA, the model was trained on approximately 2 million image tiles. This dataset includes more than one million high-resolution camera images with a resolution of 1 meter, as well as approximately 964 thousand multispectral images.

The model also draws on data from NASA's GRAIL and Lunar Prospector missions and the Japan Space Exploration Agency's (JAXA) SELENE mission.

Not Only Mapping the Moon

One of the important features of the model is that it is not tied to a single task.

In traditional methods, researchers may have to create and train a separate AI model for a specific problem. In the foundation model approach, the system is pre-trained on a large dataset and can then be adapted for different scientific tasks.

In this way, the same basic model; It can be used in different fields, from crater detection to ice research, from examining volcanic structures to determining surface changes.

Why Is It Important For People Going To The Moon In The Future?

The Moon is not expected to be a celestial body where only scientific research is carried out in the future.

Within the scope of NASA's Artemis program, it is aimed to send humans to the Moon again and to develop the infrastructure for long-term lunar research.

In this process, it is of great importance to determine safe landing zones, map the surface in detail and find potential sources.

AI-powered mapping systems can help researchers evaluate vast amounts of data in a shorter amount of time, accelerating these studies.

Why is it important to be open source?

NASA and IBM's model was not only left as a system closed to the use of institutions.

The model was made available to researchers with an open-source approach. In addition, datasets and evaluation collections that can be used for machine learning were also published.

This could allow scientists around the world to test, improve, and create new uses for the model.

The Future of Space Exploration: Human + Artificial Intelligence

The amount of data collected in space exploration is growing every year.

Next-generation telescopes, satellites, rovers, and space missions can produce billions of images and measurements. It is not possible for humans to analyze all of this data alone.

Therefore, artificial intelligence is becoming a tool that helps scientists make sense of larger amounts of data faster, rather than being a technology that replaces them.

The NASA-IBM Lunar Foundation Model is one of the important examples of this transformation in lunar research.

Perhaps the first thing we will look at when exploring unknown regions of the Moon in the future will not only be telescope images, but also the conclusions drawn by artificial intelligence from these images.

Conclusion

NASA and IBM's new lunar artificial intelligence model shows that space exploration and artificial intelligence are increasingly intersecting.

Studies such as investigating ice on the Moon, mapping craters and understanding the geological history of the surface are now based not only on human observation but also on big data and artificial intelligence technologies.

As preparations for a return to the Moon accelerate, not only rockets and spacecraft, but also artificial intelligence will play an important role in future space exploration.
