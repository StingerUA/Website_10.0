ALBA Space Blog Post - İdris ALBAYRAK FIRSTS IN SPACE - 21.08.2026 Greetings to space, spacers! Firsts are unforgettable. Firsts are pioneers. Firsts are brave, and courage is the only thing that cannot be imitated. The most unforgettable and successful firsts in human history have been made with discoveries in the history of science. Let's take a look at the little-known firsts in space together. THE FIRST LIVING THING IN SPACE Fruit flies were the first animals sent into space. They were blown up in 1947 aboard a V-2 rocket and reached an altitude of about 200 miles in less than 68 seconds before returning to Earth by parachute. THE FIRST LIVING CREATURE GROWN IN LUNAR SOIL Scientists grew plants in lunar soil samples brought to Earth during the Apollo 11, Apollo 12 and Apollo 17 Moon Missions. The plant, whose Latin name is Arabidopsis Thaliana and known as Mouse Ear Cress, was experimented on 3 different soils. Although the plants also sprouted in the soil called JSC-1A, which NASA prepared to mimic the lunar soil, the plants grown in the Apollo 17 soil, which was taken from the region exposed to the least solar wind and cosmic rays, sprouted healthier than the others among these three samples. Plants that sprouted in JSC-1A soil, on the other hand, grew significantly faster and healthier than the others. Since the genetic codes of the Arabidopsis Thaliana plant were extracted 100%, genetic deterioration in the plants was also detected. THE FIRST MAN-MADE OBJECT TO GO OUT OF THE SOLAR SYSTEM Launched on September 5, 1977, NASA/JPL's LC-41 with a Titan IIIE/Centaur rocket from the Cape Canaveral ramp and its mission; The first object to study the extraplanetary solar wind and interstellar exploration is the Voyager-1 space probe. In addition, Voyager-1 also contains gramophone records called Voyager Gold Records. The record contains selected sounds and images that show the diversity of life and cultures on Earth, with the intention of finding extraterrestrial intelligent life forms or future humans. The Turkish audio recording on the records contains the phrase "Dear Turkish-speaking friends, may your morning sheriffs be good". After 40,000 years, Voyager-1 will reach the star AC+79 3888, located 17.6 light-years away in the constellation Giraffe. THE FIRST PHOTO TAKEN ON THE SURFACE OF THE PLANET MARS The first photo taken on the surface of the planet Mars; It was acquired by the Viking 1 spacecraft, just minutes after its successful landing on Mars on July 20, 1976. THE FIRST HELICOPTER TO FLY ON A PLANET OTHER THAN EARTH The first helicopter to land on Mars on February 18, 2021 in NASA's Perseverance rover; Ingenuity. This helicopter, which means "Creativity" in Turkish, first flew over Mars on April 19, 2021. It was the first helicopter to fly outside the world. Normally, Ingenuity was scheduled to operate on Mars for about 30 Martian days (about 40 minutes longer than Earth's day). However, due to the success of Ingenuity in its first three flights, the mission period was extended by NASA for another 30 Martian days. THE FIRST ASTEROID TO BE SAMPLED The space probe named Hayabusa-2 by JAXA was launched from the Tanegashima Space Center on December 3, 2014 at 04:22 UTC. After a three-and-a-half-year journey, it reached the Ryugu meteorite, which is 280 million kilometers away, in July 2018. On February 22, 2019, it began the landing procedure and was preceded by launching a 300-gram dust-raising projectile reaching a speed of 5 meters per second to the surface to facilitate sample collection. After landing on the meteorite, it collected dust samples from the environment using a sample collection device. TURKEY'S FIRST CUBE SATELLITE UBAKUSAT is a cube satellite developed by Istanbul Technical University. It was launched into space on April 2, 2018, aboard a Falcon-9 rocket, and was dropped from the International Space Station on May 11, 2018, and placed into orbit. It was built to provide voice communications for amateur radio stations and as an earth observation satellite. THE FIRST PIPE KEBAB SENT FROM ADANA TO THE STRATOSPHERE LAYER On April 12, 2022, the 61st anniversary of Yuri Gagarin, the first man in space, the ALBA LOON Space Exploration team from Adana broke new ground in the world in cooperation with Adana's global brand Ribcı Yaşar Usta. With a helium balloon, 485 gr. Boru Kebab is 38 km from the ground. He sent it to the height, to the stratosphere layer. They recorded the entire journey with an action camera and tracked the payload with the help of GPS. ~120 km from launch. They reached the payload that made a soft landing in the sea with a parachute off the coast of Dörtyol, Hatay and delivered it to Yaşar Aydın. All Spacers who go to Adana can see the world's first stratosphere Pipe Kebab exhibited in the restaurant.
