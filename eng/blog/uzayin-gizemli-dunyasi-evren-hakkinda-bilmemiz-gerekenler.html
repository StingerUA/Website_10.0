The Mysterious World of Space: What We Need to Know About the Universe

Muzaffer Enes İpçi · Guest Writer · August 28, 2026 · 7 min read

The Mysterious World of Space: What We Need to Know About the Universe Throughout history, humanity has tried to understand its place in the universe by looking at the sky. This process, ranging from a few thousand stars visible to the naked eye to advanced space telescopes capable of studying billions of galaxies, has greatly changed our knowledge of the universe. Today, we know significantly how planets form, how stars are born and die, and how black holes behave. However, the universe still holds many unanswered questions. But how big is space? Could there be life on other planets? How do black holes form? Could humanity live on other planets in the future? In this article, we discuss the most remarkable features of space and the important questions that scientists are working on. What is Space? Space is the vast environment beyond Earth's atmosphere that houses countless cosmic structures such as planets, stars, galaxies, asteroids, comets, and clouds of gas and dust. Space is not completely empty. It contains extremely low densities of particles, gases, electromagnetic radiation, and various cosmic substances. One of the most striking features of space is its enormous scale. The kilometers we use in daily life are insufficient to express cosmic distances. Therefore, astronomers use units of measurement such as light years to describe much larger distances. What is a Light Year? A light year is not a unit of time, but a unit of distance. It refers to the distance that light travels in a vacuum for a year. Light travels at a speed of about 300 thousand kilometers per second. Despite this, it takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth from the Sun. Light from more distant stars can take years, thousands of years, or even millions of years to reach Earth. Therefore, looking at a distant celestial body in astronomy also means looking into its history. For example, when we observe a star 1,000 light-years away, we see light that departed from that star about 1,000 years ago. 1 Where is Earth in the Universe? Earth is the only planet in the Solar System where life is known. The Solar System is located within the Milky Way Galaxy. The Milky Way is thought to contain hundreds of billions of stars. The Sun is just one of these stars. Moreover, the Milky Way is just one of the countless galaxies in the observable universe. On this scale, it can be better understood how small the Earth's physical position in the universe is. Despite this, Earth is the only place where life originated as far as we know. Exoplanets: Worlds Beyond the Solar System Planets located outside the Solar System are called exoplanets. Since the first exoplanet discoveries, scientists have identified thousands of planets. The characteristics of these planets are quite different from each other. Some have similar dimensions to Earth, while others are gas giants much larger than Jupiter. Some are so close to their stars that their surface conditions can be extremely hot. Scientists are particularly interested in planets that are in the habitable zone of their stars. The habitable zone refers to the region around a star where temperatures suitable for liquid water to exist on the surface can occur. However, just because a planet is in the habitable zone does not mean that there is definitely life there. Is There Life in Space? One of the biggest questions in space exploration is whether there is life outside of Earth. So far, there is no confirmed evidence of life outside of Earth. However, scientists are investigating many celestial bodies that may have suitable conditions for life. Mars is one of the most important targets of these researches. In the past, strong evidence has been obtained that liquid water existed on the surface of Mars. Jupiter's moon Europa and Saturn's Enceladus moon also attract the attention of scientists. There is significant evidence that liquid water oceans may exist beneath the icy surfaces of these moons. The main purpose of these researches is not only to find life, but also to understand the conditions under which life can emerge in the universe. How Do Black Holes Form? Black holes are cosmic structures with extraordinarily strong gravity. 2 Not even light can escape from within a certain boundary of a black hole. This boundary is called the event horizon. Some black holes can form as a result of processes that occur at the end of the lives of very massive stars. In addition, there are supermassive black holes that can reach millions or billions of solar masses at the centers of galaxies. It is not possible to see black holes directly. However, we can detect their presence by observing their effects on the matter and light around them. How Big is the Universe? We don't know for sure how big the entire universe is. The region that scientists can observe is called the observable universe. Its diameter is estimated to be about 93 billion light years. The important point here is that although the universe is only 13.8 billion years old, the diameter of the observable universe is much larger than 13.8 billion light years. This is due to the expansion of the universe. As light travels towards us, space itself continues to expand. The Universe Is Expanding Observations made in the 1920s revealed that distant galaxies are generally moving away from each other. These observations were one of the important pieces of evidence showing that the universe is expanding. Today, it is accepted that the universe began to expand from a very hot and dense state about 13.8 billion years ago. The model that explains this process is called the Big Bang model. The Big Bang should not be thought of as an ordinary explosion in space. What is at stake here is the expansion of space itself. Dark Matter and Dark Energy A significant part of what we know about the universe consists of structures that we cannot actually see directly. Since dark matter does not interact directly with light, it cannot be directly observed with telescopes. However, there is strong evidence for its existence through the movements of galaxies and gravitational effects. Dark energy, on the other hand, is a concept associated with the acceleration of the expansion of the universe and its nature is still not fully understood. These two topics are among the largest research areas in modern cosmology. 3 Why are Space Telescopes Important? The Earth's atmosphere blocks or alters some electromagnetic radiation from space. Therefore, making some observations from space provides a great advantage. Thanks to space telescopes, important information about the formation of stars, the development of galaxies, the atmospheres of exoplanets and the early universe can be obtained. Advanced infrared observations, in particular, help us study distant and ancient cosmic structures that were previously difficult to see. The Future of Humanity in Space Space exploration is not limited to scientific discoveries. In the future, it is aimed to carry out longer-duration manned missions on the Moon, examine Mars in more detail and develop new space technologies. However, reaching other star systems is an extremely big challenge for today's technology. For example, Alpha Centauri, one of the closest star systems to the Sun, is about 4.37 light-years away. It would take a very long time to cover this distance with spacecraft used today. Therefore, faster propulsion systems, advanced robotic technologies and long-duration manned missions may play an important role in future space exploration. There is still a lot we don't know about space Although scientists have solved many of the secrets of space, there are many questions waiting to be answered. How big is the entire universe? What is the true structure of dark matter? Why does dark energy accelerate the expansion of the universe? Is there life outside Earth? How did the first galaxies form? How did supermassive black holes come about? The answers to these questions could change not only astronomy but also our overall understanding of the universe. Conclusion Space is one of the greatest scientific fields that humanity continues to explore. Each new telescope, spacecraft and research method reveals new information about the universe that we did not know before. 4 Today, we have a much better understanding of Earth's place in the universe than we did in the past. However, the knowledge we have is still very limited compared to what can be discovered. Perhaps a future discovery will change the way we think about life outside Earth or reshape our current knowledge of how the universe was formed. Understanding space is not just about investigating distant planets and stars. It is also about trying to understand the universe we live in and our own existence. 5
