That is the power of science, Spacemates... It brings people together. The station was actually launched into space piece by piece, almost like a gigantic LEGO set. 🧩🚀 Astronauts assembled those pieces while floating high above Earth. Just imagine it... People working hundreds of kilometers above our planet, building a giant laboratory in the vacuum of space! 🌌 Inside the ISS, astronauts live, sleep, eat, exercise, and perform scientific experiments. 👨‍🔬🧪 But living in space is not easy. Because there is very little gravity aboard the station. Astronauts cannot walk normally... They float everywhere! 😄 If they drop a pencil, it doesn't fall. Researchers use the ISS to find answers to questions like these. And did you know, Spacemates...
